> There was once a small kitchen where a grandmother taught her grandson to watch a pot. “See how the water sits so quietly at first,” she said. “It holds its peace, and nothing seems to happen. >
> In another house, a farmer rose before dawn to check the kettle. He did not demand that the fire hurry; he simply fed it, and trusted the slow work of heat. “The world has its own quiet timetables,” he told his daughter. “We are not the ones who set the moment of rising. We are only the ones who keep the flame and watch with wonder.” And so they sat together, listening to the soft hum of the pot.
>
> Scripture speaks of seeds that grow while the sower sleeps, of leaven hidden in dough until the whole batch is lifted. >
> May you find grace in the quiet watching, and peace in the slow rising of good things. Amen.
